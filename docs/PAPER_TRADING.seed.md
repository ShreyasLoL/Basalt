2. **Separation of External Market Data vs. Internal Matching Engine (`PAPER_TRADING.md`)**:
* **Rule 1**: External NSE/BSE quotes from Upstox/FYERS are **observations**, never treated as resting orders inside BASALT's internal C++ `OrderBook` unless explicitly routed through the `OrderBookReplayExecutionModel`.

* **Rule 2**: BASALT supports **4 explicit Paper Execution Models** selectable per strategy/account:

1. `CONSERVATIVE_QUOTE_MODEL` (Default for Live/Delayed Provider Feeds): Market buys fill at $\text{Ask} + \text{Slippage}_{\text{ticks}}$, market sells fill at $\text{Bid} - \text{Slippage}_{\text{ticks}}$ (never optimistically at LTP). Limit buys fill only when $\text{Ask} \le P_{\text{limit}}$.Fill quantity is capped by visible top-of-book quantity (`bid_qty` / `ask_qty`).

2. `BAR_APPROXIMATION_MODEL` (For OHLC Historical Backtests): Orders emitted on Bar $t$ close execute at Bar $t+1$ Open $\pm$ half-spread/slippage penalty; intra-bar limit orders fill only if Bar $t+1$ High/Low strictly breaches the limit price, capped at configurable volume participation (default `10%` of bar volume).

3. `DEPTH_REPLAY_MODEL` (For 5-level or 20-level Recorded Depth Streams): Sweeps observed depth rungs with configurable latency delay $\Delta t_{\text{sim}}$ (e.g., `5 ms`), computing volume-weighted average execution price across rungs.

4. `INTERNAL_CLOB_MODEL` (Exchange Simulation Mode): Routes orders directly into BASALT's C++ `MatchingEngine` where user orders, strategy orders, and explicit synthetic market-maker agents interact under strict price-time priority.
