1. **Authoritative Fixed-Point Representation (`include/basalt/common/types.hpp`)**:
* Every monetary value and price uses `int64_t` scaled by $S = 10,000$ (`1.0000 INR = 10,000` micro-units; `0.05 INR` NSE tick = `500` micro-units).
* Notional value of a trade of quantity $Q$ (integer shares) at price $P_{\text{micro}}$ is:

$$\text{Notional}_{\text{micro}} = P_{\text{micro}} \times Q$$

* Using `int64_t` (max $\approx 9.22 \times 10^{18}$ micro-units $\approx ₹9.22 \times 10^{14}$) eliminates floating-point rounding drift and protects against 32-bit overflow; `__int128` is used for intermediate multiplication before scaling division.

3. **Portfolio Accounting & Double-Entry Cash Ledger (`DATA_MODEL.md`)**:
* For each account $a$ and instrument $i$, the ledger tracks signed position quantity $q_{a,i} \in \mathbb{Z}$, average entry cost in micro-units $\bar{c}_{a,i}$, realized P&L $R_{a,i}$, cumulative fees $F_{a,i}$, and cash balance $C_a$:

* **Opening or Increasing Position** ($\text{sign}(q_{\text{fill}}) = \text{sign}(q_{a,i})$ or $q_{a,i} = 0$):

$$\bar{c}'_{a,i} = \frac{|q_{a,i}| \cdot \bar{c}_{a,i} + |q_{\text{fill}}| \cdot P_{\text{exec}}}{|q_{a,i}| + |q_{\text{fill}}|}, \quad q'_{a,i} = q_{a,i} + q_{\text{fill}}$$

* **Reducing or Closing Position** ($|q_{\text{fill}}| \le |q_{a,i}|$ and opposite sign):

$$\Delta R = |q_{\text{fill}}| \cdot (P_{\text{exec}} - \bar{c}_{a,i}) \cdot \text{sign}(q_{a,i}), \quad R'_{a,i} = R_{a,i} + \Delta R, \quad \bar{c}'_{a,i} = \bar{c}_{a,i}$$

* **Position Flip** ($|q_{\text{fill}}| > |q_{a,i}|$ and opposite sign): Split deterministically into TWO ledger legs—Leg 1 closes $q_{\text{close}} = -q_{a,i}$ at $P_{\text{exec}}$ realizing $\Delta R$; Leg 2 opens the residual $q_{\text{open}} = q_{\text{fill}} - q_{\text{close}}$ with new average cost $\bar{c}'_{a,i} = P_{\text{exec}}$.

* **Conservative Unrealized P&L**: Long positions mark-to-market against current `best_bid` (or LTP if quotes unavailable); Short positions mark-to-market against current `best_ask`:

$$U_{a,i} = \begin{cases} q_{a,i} \cdot (P_{\text{bid}} - \bar{c}_{a,i}) & \text{if } q_{a,i} > 0 \\ |q_{a,i}| \cdot (\bar{c}_{a,i} - P_{\text{ask}}) & \text{if } q_{a,i} < 0 \\ 0 & \text{if } q_{a,i} = 0 \end{cases}$$
